📌 Scope & Statistical Conventions

Dataset: N = 4,001 employees (cross-sectional, single time point)
Significance threshold: For N=4,001, Pearson r must exceed |r| > 0.031 to reach p<0.05 (two-tailed). All Q1 correlations (r=0.002 to 0.038) are near this boundary or below it.
Effect size guide: Cohen’s d <0.2 = negligible, 0.2–0.5 = small, 0.5–0.8 = medium, >0.8 = large
Observational data: All findings are associational. No causal claims are made without explicit qualification.
LLM role: Narrative, weighting logic, and composite scoring formulas are LLM-generated. Raw correlations and counts are Python-computed from source data.

Section 1 — Unified Insight Framework
Connecting Q1 through Q5 into a single analytical narrative
🎯 Q1 Finding

Skills (Tech, Comm, ProbSolve) have negligible correlation with performance. Communication is the strongest predictor at r=0.038 — but context matters more than skill.

🚀 Q2 Finding

36.3% of high performers show low leadership potential — the "Silent Contributor" segment with 45% attrition risk. Needs a non-traditional retention and recognition strategy.

⚡ Q3 Finding

Training Efficiency is the #1 differentiator — 3× gap (0.244 vs 0.088). Low performers are not unskilled; they are inefficient. Context drives performance, not raw ability.

🔍 Q4 Finding

346 high-skill employees (8.6%) are associated with project failures. Burnout, engagement deficits, and role-complexity mismatches explain anomalies — not skill gaps.

🌟 Q5 Finding

Ideal employees are defined by efficient skill application, not skill superiority. Composite Score = Skills + Soft Skills + Training Efficiency + Engagement + WLB.

💎 Unifying Truth

"Performance is not a function of ability alone, but of how effectively that ability is leveraged within an organizational environment — shaped by efficiency, engagement, well-being and alignment."

Section Challenges & Solutions
Differentiator view: converting Section 1 insights into execution moves
CHALLENGE
Direct skill scores have weak linear signal for performance; skill-only evaluations miss context.
SOLUTION
Adopt a context-aware scorecard combining skill, engagement, workload, and delivery outcomes.
CHALLENGE
High-skill yet failed-outcome anomalies hide burnout, role mismatch, and team-friction risk.
SOLUTION
Run anomaly triage reviews with managers using overtime, WLB, and project context before reassignment.
CHALLENGE
High performance and leadership potential diverge for a large cohort, creating promotion ambiguity.
SOLUTION
Use dual career tracks: specialist growth path and leadership readiness path with explicit criteria.
